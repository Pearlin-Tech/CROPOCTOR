 🌱 CropDoctor

AI-powered crop health assistant for smarter, faster agricultural decisions.

> Take a photo. Detect the problem. Understand the disease. Protect the crop.**

CropDoctor is an AI-powered agricultural platform designed to help farmers and growers identify crop diseases from plant images and understand what they can do next.

Instead of relying on complicated agricultural terminology or waiting for an expert, CropDoctor turns a simple crop image into an understandable diagnosis with practical guidance.

---

🚜 Why CropDoctor?

A disease appearing on a crop can quickly become a serious problem.

The challenge isn't only **detecting the disease** — it's knowing:

- What is affecting the crop?
- How serious is it?
- What caused it?
- What should be done now?
- How can it be prevented next time?

CropDoctor brings these answers together in one simple interface.

---

##✨ What It Does

 📸 AI Crop Diagnosis
Upload or capture an image of an affected crop leaf and let the AI analyze visible symptoms.

 🧠 Disease Detection
The system analyzes the image and identifies the most likely crop disease or health condition.

 🔬 Explainable Results
Instead of returning only a disease name, CropDoctor aims to explain the result in a way that is easier to understand.

 💊 Treatment Guidance
Get practical suggestions for managing the detected condition.

 🛡️ Prevention
Understand how the disease can be prevented from spreading or returning.

 🌾 Crop Health Assistance
Use the platform as a digital first layer of assistance before consulting an agricultural expert.
